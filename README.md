## ⚡ The Person Behind the Code

I'm **Sean**, a Mechatronics Engineering student with a minor in **Data Science**.

I'm fascinated by intelligent machines, robotics, and high-performance engineering. Particularly, how mechanical design, electronics, software, and data come together to push the limits of what's possible.

I enjoy the challenge of understanding complex systems, finding patterns others might overlook, and turning information into something useful.

To me, engineering is about asking a simple question:

**"It works. But how much better could it work?"**

## 🏎️ The Formula 1 Obsession

I've been following **Formula 1 since 2008**.

What fascinates me most isn't simply the racing. It's the engineering happening behind every lap.

Aerodynamics, powertrain efficiency, vehicle dynamics, energy management, control systems, simulation, and mountains of telemetry. All working together to find fractions of a second.

**Give me a telemetry trace, a performance graph, or a dataset full of anomalies, and I'll happily spend hours figuring out what the numbers are trying to tell me.**

That's the kind of thinking I want to bring into engineering.

> "You are always looking for that extra bit of performance, that extra bit of downforce, that extra bit of efficiency. It's a never-ending quest."
>
> **Adrian Newey - Oracle Redbull Racing**

For me, Formula 1 represents what happens when extraordinary engineering meets relentless innovation.
